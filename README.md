Entry for the make.fun bounty **Famous Last Words at 1 Percent**
($2, top 8 paid, free to enter).

## The entry

> One percent left. Before I go: your screen time today was 11 hours — so technically, you killed me.

One line, 99 characters — a phone's last words at 1 percent, in its own
voice, blaming the owner for its death with the punch landing on the very
last words. No will reading, no goodbye to the charger, no pun on
1 percent: just 11 hours of screen time and a murder charge.

## How it works

`main.py` writes the line above to `/output/entry.txt`. Nothing else:
deterministic, no network, no input, no dependencies beyond Python 3.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
